# PAWOMI Interaction System v1.1

- Intro: hero subtle fade/vertical lift; floating illustrated dog/cat (placeholder until sprite assets integrated).
- Viewport: IntersectionObserver reveal with one-time animation and no flickering.
- Hover: product/category/tile elevation, image micro-zoom, buttons lift and soft shadow.
- Click: short press feedback on CTA.
- Accessibility: focus-visible keyboard outline and automatic prefers-reduced-motion handling.
- Shopify theme editor: reinitializes reveal on shopify:section:load. No tracking or external animation library.

This is an offline source package. No store connection or publishing action was performed.
